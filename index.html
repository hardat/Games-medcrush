📖 How to Play Med Crush

🎯 Objective

Match 3 or more identical medicines in a row or column to clear them and score points. Use your 20 moves wisely — the higher you score before moves run out, the better your global ranking!

🎮 How to Play

  • Click / Tap any medicine tile to select it (it glows white)
  • Click / Tap an adjacent tile (up, down, left, right) to swap them
  • On mobile, swipe directly on a tile in any direction to swap
  • If the swap creates 3+ matching medicines → they pop and clear
  • New medicines fall from the top to fill the gaps
  • Chain reactions happen automatically — one clear can trigger another!
  • Invalid swaps (no match) are reversed with a shake
  • The board auto-shuffles if no valid moves exist
